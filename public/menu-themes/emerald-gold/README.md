# Emerald Gold menu theme

Content pages use `background-content-v1.png`, an imagegen edit with the fixed food photography removed. Category photos are loaded dynamically from `public/menu-category-images` beside their own dishes; the original cover artwork is retained. Static theme previews are rendered from the updated sample PDF.

Select **Emerald Gold** in the Download PDF dialog. The cover appears once on page 1, followed by separate portrait menu pages with up to three category blocks each. Longer lists continue across blocks/pages with their category number retained. The theme uses gold logo lettering, gold numbering, cream serif text, locally bundled Playball script, and the reference's “Timeless Favourites / Always a Good Idea” footer caption.

The artwork was created from the supplied reference using the built-in image generator. `background-v1.png` is 1536 × 1024; each panel is clipped into its own 300 × 600 point PDF page during rendering. Fonts approximate the raster reference, whose original font metadata is unavailable.

Generate a sample with `node --import tsx scripts/preview-botanical-menu.ts --emerald`. Add `--stress` to verify the full catalog.

## Artwork prompt

Produce a high-resolution text-free production background for dynamic PDF text overlays. Maintain exact wide 3:2 aspect ratio and three equal vertical panels, thin gold outer frames and panel divisions, realistic olive-green/gold botanical corners, dark emerald green velvet/marble background, gold candle bokeh, black marble platters, premium food photography and original image placement. Remove ALL text including the logo, TM, Jai Mata Di, tagline, menu title, all heading numbers, category headings, selection labels, dish lists, script quotes and footer labels. Remove all bullet symbols, category icons, cover top lotus icon and bottom event icons. Seamlessly fill those locations with clean dark emerald background. Preserve the canape platter across the lower third of the left panel, with its upper half blank and clear dark space at right around 50-59% height for a dynamic script quote. Keep drinks upper right of middle panel, dark soup bowl center right, paneer platter lower right; veg starters upper right of right panel, spinach rolls center right, gold halwa bowl lower right. Leave clear dark text zones along the left side of the middle and right panels, unobstructed by bright steam, foliage, or bokeh. Leave clear dark horizontal heading bands at 9%, 37%, and 60% height so long dynamic headings remain legible. Keep food toward right edge of content panels. Retain the bottom botanical corners and clear footer spaces. No lettering or watermarks anywhere, no new logos, flat full-bleed artwork without perspective or external shadows. Match the image's elegant forest-green, gold and warm candlelit mood.
