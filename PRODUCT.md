# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Back-office catering admins and kitchen staff managing raw materials, vendor assignments, event orders, and menus. Primary context is an **iPad** at the venue or kitchen office (Safari or home-screen shortcut); phone is secondary, desktop tertiary. Tasks are quick and operational — assign suppliers, check catalog, log orders, pull a menu PDF.

## Product Purpose

A tablet-first catering operations app: one place to run materials, vendors, orders, and menus without switching tools. Success looks like staff completing an assignment or order on an iPad in a few taps, without hunting through dense desktop admin UI.

## Positioning

A proper iPad-ready catering ops app — built for Safari (or home screen), not a shrunk desktop panel.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, unobtrusive. A tool that disappears into the workflow. Fresh teal accent — clear in presentations, never decorative.

## Anti-references

- Marketing landing page aesthetics (hero metrics, gradient text, cream/sand backgrounds)
- Dense desktop-only admin panels
- Hamburger-only navigation on mobile without a clear home path back

## Design Principles

- **iPad first** — layouts, touch targets, and flows assume tablet; phone and desktop adapt down/up
- **Ops speed over density** — one primary job per screen; favor scannable lists and clear actions
- **Destinations, not decoration** — navigation reads as places to go, not content cards
- **Readable in real kitchens** — contrast and type hold up under bright lights and at arm's length

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Sensible defaults: keyboard/focus affordances, sufficient contrast, and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`. Hard requirements for this context: **large tap targets** (gloves / wet hands) and **high contrast / outdoor-and-kitchen readability**.
