# Catering Demo — Product Context

## Register

product

## Users & Purpose

Back-office catering admins managing raw materials, vendor assignments, and event orders. Primary devices: phone and tablet at venue or kitchen office. Tasks are quick and operational — assign suppliers, check catalog, log orders.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, unobtrusive. A tool that disappears into the workflow. Warm but not decorative.

## Anti-References

- Marketing landing page aesthetics (hero metrics, gradient text, cream/sand backgrounds)
- Dense desktop-only admin panels
- Hamburger-only navigation on mobile without a clear home path back

## Strategic Design Principles

- Mobile and tablet first; desktop is secondary
- Restrained color: pure white surfaces, one warm terracotta accent
- Lucide icons for consistent modern affordances
- Navigation tiles read as destinations, not content cards
